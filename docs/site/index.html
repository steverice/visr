VISR

Halo: Combat Evolved for Apple devices

VISR is a native port of Halo: Combat Evolved for Apple Vision Pro and other Apple devices: iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Mac. Built from the Halo: CE decompilation, it renders on-device with Metal.

The goal is to optimize Halo for Apple hardware while staying faithful to the original game.

GitHub · Roadmap

Apple Vision Pro

  • Full stereo VR with head tracking, through Compositor Services
  • Foveated rendering through the Compositor's rate maps
  • The HUD in CE's own corners, out in the periphery
  • Scope zoom fills the view
  • Third-person cutscenes on a 3D screen in the room

VISR also plays in 2D, in a window or on a virtual screen in the room.

iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Mac

  • iPhone and iPad: landscape play with on-screen controls or a game controller. Wired external-display output keeps the HUD and touch controls on the device.
  • Apple TV: controller play on the TV, with graphics profiles for Apple TV HD and Apple TV 4K models.
  • Mac: a native Mac app, with capped or uncapped rendering.

Device profiles choose graphics settings for the hardware, with separate budgets for gameplay, menus and cutscenes. MetalFX upscaling, FXAA and SMAA are available. On flat screens, the HUD and menus render at display resolution even when the scene renders below it.

The original intro and credits movies play from the imported disc.

Multiplayer and saves

Split-screen co-op, LAN play and internet play come from OpenCE. Invite links open in VISR across the Apple platforms. Internet play asks before contacting third-party services. Bonjour discovery and the invite share sheet are available on the platforms other than Apple TV.

Optional iCloud save synchronization carries profiles and campaign saves between devices in builds configured for it.

Signed profile and benchmark-recording packs can update device tuning independently of app releases.

Get it

Installing, setup and configuration are in the README on GitHub. You need your own copy of the original Xbox game.
